Follow this 4-stage sequential blueprint to earn the GTA VI Platinum in a single seamless playthrough without locking yourself out of partner-dependent missables.

Stage 1

Main Story Campaign & Early Companion Missables

Approx. 35 - 40 Hours

Play through the main storyline across Chapters 1 through 5. Enjoy the narrative, but be extremely vigilant during Chapters 2 and 3: whenever free-roam opens up with Lucia and Jason paired together in the same vehicle, immediately knock out the companion-dependent missable trophies.

Key Milestones & Actions:
  • Sneakthief: Complete an undetected jewelry store heist during early freelance burglaries in Chapter 2.
  • Retail Therapy: Hit 3 convenience marts back-to-back with your partner while maintaining your Wanted heat in Little Haiti.
  • Hot Together: Raise your Wanted Level to 4 stars with your partner and successfully evade police via transit tunnels or spray shops.
  • Personal Best: Hang out, share heist takes, and max out dual-character synergy before Chapter 4 climax.
  • Actions Speak Louder: Create a manual backup save at the start of Chapter 4 before executing the syndicate informant decision.
Stage 2

Strangers & Freaks Side Mission Strands

Approx. 12 - 15 Hours

Venture across Leonida's orange '?' icons on your radar. Complete all sequential storyline strands for at least 8 unique characters.

Key Strands:
  • Andy (Road to Rehabilitation): Conclude the parole officer sting operations in Port Gellhorn.
  • Kenton (This Was No Boat Accident): Complete underwater wreckage salvage contracts in the Keys.
  • Billy (The Lone Rider): Defend outlaw biker territory in the western county badlands.
  • Randy (Supply & Demand): Escort agricultural contraband air-drops in Ambrosia.
  • Tyrone (Spin On This!): Master gymkhana drift trials at the Vice Beach industrial container terminal.
Stage 3

Collectibles, Exploration & 100% Completion Checklist

Approx. 20 - 25 Hours

Systematically clear map fog and collect all world items necessary for the Gold trophy A Long Road.

Checklist Objectives:
  • You WAiNKer!: Take geotagged smartphone selfies at 100 landmarks across the state.
  • Don't Touch My Deck: Assemble 3 full 20-card collectible card sets.
  • Cunning Stunts: Land 250 stunt jumps, billboard ramps, and canal leaps using a modified Pegassi Bati 801.
  • Positive Mental Attitude: Meditate at all 15 scenic viewpoints across Leonida.
  • The Goodtime State: Uncover 100% of the world map fog of war.
  • Little Boxes: Buy all 7 garage properties on Dynasty 8 Real Estate.
  • Whip It Good: Sit behind the wheel of 100 distinct vehicle classes.
Stage 4

Combat Cleanup, Activities & Platinum Victory

Approx. 8 - 10 Hours

Mop up remaining miscellaneous combat milestones, minigames, and syndicate challenges.

Cleanup Checkpoints:
  • The Shootist: Earn gold medals in all Ammu-Nation firing range weapons categories.
  • Top Fun: Win 1st place in all 6 drone race courses across Leonida.
  • Earn Cash, Not Kudos: Win 20 sanctioned street, off-road, and water races.
  • Gunslinger: Score 5 headshot kills in a single Combat Focus activation.
  • Dime on the Dollar: Launder $250,000 in marked robbery currency through fronts.
  • Let's Go Bowling: Roll a Turkey (3 consecutive strikes) at Kolt & Associates.
  • Vice Got a Price!: Collect your well-earned Platinum Trophy!
